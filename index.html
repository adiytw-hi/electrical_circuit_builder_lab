Assumptions & safe use

What the simulator includes

Ideal DC voltage source, ideal wires, ideal open/closed switches, positive-value linear resistors, Kirchhoff’s current and voltage laws, steady-state current, and electrical power dissipated in resistors. The meter is ideal and non-invasive.

Battery shorts are flagged and numerical current/power is intentionally withheld. It does not simulate internal resistance, fuses, parasitic effects, capacitance, inductance, real components’ temperature, or the hazardous consequences of real wiring.

Accessibility: use the component selector and arrow buttons to move components; select terminals from dropdowns to wire them; all quiz questions have text feedback. Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Z for Undo; Delete for selected wire/component when not typing.

ElectroLab is a conceptual low-voltage educational model. Never connect real batteries, power supplies, or components based solely on its results. No account, analytics, server, or external dependencies.
